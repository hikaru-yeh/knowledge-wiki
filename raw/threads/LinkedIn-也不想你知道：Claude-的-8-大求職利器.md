THAT’S WHY LINKEDIN DOESN’T WANT YOU USING CLAUDE🔥
My nephew applied to 47 jobs on LinkedIn. Got 2 callbacks.
Gave him these prompts instead. Got 6 interviews in 11 days.
No premium. No recruiter. No resume blasting.
Here are 8 prompts that changed everything↓

1 ▸ Find Hidden Job Markets
“Act as a recruitment expert. I’m a [Job Title] with [X] years of experience in [Industry]. Find me companies actively hiring for my profile that are NOT posting on LinkedIn or Indeed. Focus on companies that are growing, recently funded, or expanding into new markets.”

2 ▸ Reverse Engineer the Job Description
“Here is a job description I want to apply for: [Paste JD]. Identify the 10 most important skills and keywords the hiring manager is actually looking for. Then rewrite my resume bullet points to match them without lying about my experience.”

3 ▸ Cold Outreach That Gets Replies
“Write me a cold outreach message to the hiring manager at [Company] for a [Role] position. Do not make it sound like a cover letter. Make it sound like a sharp professional reaching out to a peer. Under 100 words. End with one low-friction question.”

4 ▸ Uncover Unadvertised Roles
“Research [Company Name] and tell me which departments are likely understaffed or growing based on recent news, funding rounds, product launches, or leadership hires. Suggest which roles I should pitch proactively even if nothing is posted.”

5 ▸ Interview Prep in 20 Minutes
“I have an interview at [Company] for [Role] in [X] days. Give me the 10 most likely questions they will ask, the ideal answer structure for each, and two questions I should ask them that will make me stand out from every other candidate.”

6 ▸ Salary Negotiation Script
“I’ve received an offer of [Amount] for [Role] at [Company]. Based on current market rates for this position in [Location], am I being underpaid? Write me a negotiation script I can use on the call to counter without sounding difficult or ungrateful.”

7 ▸ LinkedIn Profile Audit
“Audit my LinkedIn profile for a [Job Title] role in [Industry]. Here is my current summary and experience section: [Paste]. Tell me exactly what to change, what keywords to add, and how to reposition my profile so recruiters find me instead of me chasing them.”

8 ▸ Full Job Search Strategy
“Act as my personal career strategist. I’m a [Role] with [X] years in [Industry] looking to move into [Target Role/Industry]. Build me a 30 day job search plan that doesn’t rely on job boards. Include outreach targets, daily actions, and exactly how to position myself to get hired faster than 95% of applicants.”
