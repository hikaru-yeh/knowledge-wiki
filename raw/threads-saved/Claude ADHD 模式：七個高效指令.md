## 主文

Claude has a hidden “ADHD Mode” and it changes everything.
Use these 7 smarter prompts instead:

1. The 5 Minute Brain Dump
“I have a million thoughts and can’t focus. Help me brain dump everything on my mind, then sort it into: do now, do later, delete. Keep responses short and punchy.”

·
Author
2. Task Untangler
“I’ve been avoiding [task] for days and I don’t know why. Break it into the smallest possible steps nothing longer than 5 minutes each. Identify what’s causing the block, whether it’s boredom, fear or overwhelm, and give me one sentence of motivation before each step to keep me moving.”

·
Author
3. Hyperfocus Hijacker
“I just fell into a hyperfocus spiral on [topic] and lost track of time. Help me capture every useful idea before it disappears, then build a structured plan to channel this energy into something productive. Include a way to bookmark where I left off so I can return to it without losing momentum.”

·
Author
4. The Body Double Bot
“Act as my virtual body double for the next [X] minutes. I need to complete [task] without getting distracted. Check in with me at regular intervals, ask what I’ve done since the last check-in, celebrate small wins out loud, and gently redirect me if I go off track. Keep the energy high and the pressure low.”

·
Author
5. Overwhelm Translator
“Everything feels urgent and I’m completely paralysed. Take this list: [paste list]. Rank everything by real urgency and impact, tell me the ONE thing I should do first and exactly why, then hide the rest so I can focus. Give me permission to ignore everything else for now.”

·
Author
6. Dopamine Task Designer
“I struggle to start and finish [boring task] because my brain refuses to engage with it. Redesign the entire experience to make it more stimulating add gamification, time challenges, rewards, novelty or competition. Give me a version I can start in the next 60 seconds without needing any extra tools or preparation.”
