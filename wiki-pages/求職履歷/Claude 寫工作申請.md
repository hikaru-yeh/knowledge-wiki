## Main Content

🚨BREAKING: Claude can now write your entire job application like a top recruiter.
Here are 10 prompts that turn a job description into a tailored CV, cover letter, and interview prep guide in under 5 minutes.
(Save this)

1. Extract what matters from the JD.
Prompt:
“Act as a recruiter. Analyze this job description and list: top skills, core responsibilities, hiring goals, must-use keywords, and what makes a standout candidate. Put it in a table. JD: [paste]”

2. Tailor my CV.
Prompt:
“Rewrite my CV for this job without inventing anything. Improve summary, experience bullets, and skills section. Use ATS-friendly keywords from the JD and make each bullet relevant and outcome-focused. CV: [paste] JD: [paste]”

3. Upgrade my bullets.
Prompt:
“Rewrite these CV bullets for this role. Use action + task + result. Keep each bullet concise, specific, and aligned to the JD. If a metric is missing, add [ADD METRIC]. Role: [title] JD: [paste] Bullets: [paste]”

4. Write a real cover letter.
Prompt:
“Write a tailored cover letter for this role using my background. Keep it clear, human, and specific. Mention 2–3 relevant achievements, connect them to the company’s needs, and avoid clichés. Background: [paste] JD: [paste]” us

5. Check my fit.
Prompt:
“Create a role-fit matrix comparing my profile to this JD. Columns: requirement, my evidence, strength level, what to stress in CV, what to address in cover letter, interview story to prepare. Background: [paste] JD: [paste]”

6. Improve ATS match.
Prompt:
“Compare my CV to this JD and show missing keywords, weak sections, bullets to improve, skills to move up, and ATS issues. Then rewrite my summary to improve match without keyword stuffing. CV: [paste] JD: [paste]”

7. Predict interview questions.
Prompt:
“Act as the hiring manager. Based on this JD and my background, generate 15 likely interview questions. Split into technical, behavioral, and fit. For each, explain why it’s asked and what a strong answer should include. Background: [paste] JD: [paste]”

8. Build STAR answers.
Prompt:
“Create 8 STAR interview answers from my background for this role. Cover leadership, problem-solving, teamwork, conflict, ownership, failure, achievement, and adaptability. Include a short interview-ready version too. Background: [paste] JD: [paste]”

9. Review my application like a recruiter.
Prompt:
“Evaluate my CV and cover letter for this role. Give a verdict: shortlist, maybe, or reject. Then explain strengths, weaknesses, and exact edits that would improve my chances fast. CV: [paste] Cover letter: [paste] JD: [paste]”10. Build the full application pack. Prompt:

10. Build the full application pack.
Prompt:
“Create a full application pack for this job: CV summary, top skills, tailored cover letter, likely interview questions, smart questions for the interviewer, recruiter DM, and follow-up email. Use only my real experience. Background: [paste] JD: [paste]”
