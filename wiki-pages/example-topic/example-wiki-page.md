# Example Wiki Page

This page demonstrates the curated wiki layer. It summarizes a source, adds structure, and can link to related pages.

## Key Points

- Raw source material stays in `raw/`.
- Curated pages live in `wiki-pages/`.
- Indexes connect durable pages into a navigable knowledge system.
